# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Humans a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and respect for differing experiences and viewpoints.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people whose data the software may hold.
- Protect private information and avoid sharing sensitive research material in public channels.

Harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, threats, doxxing, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through the hosting platform. Do not include sensitive personal data beyond what is necessary to investigate. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers who are the subject of a report must not participate in its resolution.
